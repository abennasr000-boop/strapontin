# F003 · Client review of v3 (14 Nov 2026)

## What was said
1. Better, but the design can feel lighter and more modern.
2. The grid should not be rigid and "lego-like". Imagined: full screen, more interesting size proportions, like a less exaggerated Mondrian, with text and other elements sitting on top of the images instead of in separate boxes; similar to a Cosmos preview but with more offset dimensions.
3. Clicking a film could make it bigger in the middle, with all its information and call to action, while the others shrink around it.
4. Do not recode everything: produce visual propositions to choose from.
5. Keep lightness, space and great clarity. Outlines need not be drawn; they can be implied by text alignment.
6. The current font is preferred (kept).
7. Use real posters, and a base of films giving about 7 to 12 films available today, not all visible at first glance.

## Response
Three clickable layout studies in `site/propositions.html` (A Mosaïque, B Dérive, C Accordéon). The main prototype is unchanged until one is chosen. See D024.
