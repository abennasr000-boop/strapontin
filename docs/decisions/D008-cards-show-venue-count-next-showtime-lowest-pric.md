# D008 · Cards show venue count, next showtime, lowest price

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 14 Nov 2026

## Context
K04 C2: centralisation, the core value, was invisible.

## Decision
Each film card carries "n salles · prochaine séance hh:mm · dès x €".

## Arguments
- K04: third audit priority.
- K01: "compare" is one of the three product verbs.
- K06: users currently hop between venue sites.

## Risks / what would change our mind
- Adds text under every image; watch density on mobile.

## Where it shows
Film grid, featured film, gallery reveal.
