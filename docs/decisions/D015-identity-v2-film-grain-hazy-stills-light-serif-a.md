# D015 · Identity v2: film grain, hazy stills, light serif and typewriter, lines only on actions

**Status:** Accepted (direction; details will evolve)
**Date:** 14 Nov 2026

## Context
F001: the feeling of v1 was wrong, but its clarity, its interactions and its venue illustrations were right.

## Decision
Soft paper with grain; blurred, vignetted stills in instant-photo frames; Cormorant Garamond for titles, Courier Prime for labels and data, Hanken Grotesk for paragraphs; no boxes around content; a fine ink line reserved for tickets, buttons, filters and fields; a pen-drawn fold-down seat as the mark; the circled word removed; venue façades kept inside polaroids.

## Arguments
- F001 and the client’s follow-up answers (north star, imagery, boldness, logo, type).
- K05 re-read: pins 3, 4, 6 and the gallery videos.
- K02 art-house segment: a literary, hand-made register signals cultural belonging. (Judgement, not tested.)
- K03: atmosphere is kept out of the information layer so price/time/language stay plain.
- K07: text contrast kept at 4.5:1 or better.

## Risks / what would change our mind
- Stills are generated placeholders; real images may not take the treatment well, and image rights are unresolved.
- Courier at small sizes is less legible than a sans; watch labels on mobile.
- The logo is a first sketch.
- No user from any segment has seen it.
