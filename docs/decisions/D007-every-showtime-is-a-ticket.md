# D007 · Every showtime is a ticket with price and Réserver

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 14 Nov 2026

## Context
K04 I1: showtime boxes did not look clickable and carried no price.

## Decision
One component for every bookable showtime: time and venue (or end time) on the body, full price and "Réserver" on a perforated stub.

## Arguments
- K04 I1.
- K03: price visible before commitment.
- Judgement: a ticket is a universally understood "take me" object; the tear-off hover was liked by the client (F001).

## Risks / what would change our mind
- Shows full price only; reduced fares appear at the next step. Could mislead students about what they will pay.

## Where it shows
Everywhere showtimes appear.
