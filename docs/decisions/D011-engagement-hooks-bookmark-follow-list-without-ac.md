# D011 · Engagement hooks: bookmark, follow, list without account, carnet

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 14 Nov 2026

## Context
K02: engagement rate is the critical success factor; K04 I2 found no reason to come back.

## Decision
Bookmark on every still, follow on venues and seasons, a "Ma liste" drawer stored on the device (no account needed), and the carnet as a tariff and a section.

## Arguments
- K02 success factor.
- K03: individualism (personal curation), long-term orientation (loyalty).
- K06: the CIP Ciné Carte shows shared loyalty is plausible.

## Risks / what would change our mind
- Alerts are promised but not built.
- "Engagement rate" is undefined (K02 open point), so we cannot yet say which hook matters.

## Where it shows
Cards, venue and season pages, drawer, booking step 1.
