# D026 · The test catalogue is films only, every title with a poster

**Status:** Accepted for the current test; to revisit
**Date:** 14 Nov 2026

## Context
F004 point 4: every title in the test must have a poster. The two plays and their two theatres were fictional and could only show typographic tiles.

## Decision
The test catalogue contains fifteen real films, all with a poster found on Wikipedia, across four fictional cinemas. The two fictional plays, the two theatres and the cinema/theatre filter are removed from the test.

## Arguments
- F004 point 4.
- Judgement: a mosaic that mixes real posters with placeholder tiles cannot be judged visually.

## Risks / what would change our mind
- **Theatre is part of the product brief (K01).** It is out of the test, not out of the product. It returns when real production artwork exists, and the mosaic must then be checked with landscape theatre images.
- Posters still depend on Wikipedia being reachable at view time, and are not cleared for publication (D023).
