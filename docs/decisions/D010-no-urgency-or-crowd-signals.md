# D010 · No urgency or crowd signals

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 14 Nov 2026

## Context
The reference product (BookMyShow) uses popularity and scarcity cues.

## Decision
No countdowns, stock warnings, rankings or "trending". Use critics’ quotes, festival laurels, Art et Essai labels and named programmers.

## Arguments
- K03: uncertainty avoidance, individualism, achievement scores (as given).
- K04 praised v0 for already doing this.

## Risks / what would change our mind
- May lower short-term conversion compared with urgency tactics; accepted as a brand position.

## Where it shows
Film page, programmers’ picks, special screenings.
