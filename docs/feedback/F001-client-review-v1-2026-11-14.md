# F001 · Client review of v1 (14 Nov 2026)

**From:** the client (project owner), after seeing prototype v1 and identity v1.

## What was said
1. The logo does not look like a strapontin and looks too much like an existing platform's logo.
2. The small interactions and the feel of the site are liked. The identity is not: too bold, "cartoony". The intended feeling is film grain, haze, the nostalgia of polaroids, "shot on film and written by a tormented soul" (deliberately exaggerated).
3. The clarity of the bold elements, their outlines, and the venue ("salles") designs are liked. A compromise is needed: functional *and* aligned with the feeling.
4. The red line circling one word of the title is awkward, hurts readability, has no real use.
5. The header does not appear to be sticky.
6. The palette usage is not convincing.
7. Overall: boost feel and creativity; keep clarity, readability and trust first; identity keywords: bohemian Parisian, lightness, dreaminess, discovery.
8. Process: decisions need written arguments and sources; knowledge (Hofstede, audit…) and its concrete contribution must be documented; everything goes to a private GitHub repository.
