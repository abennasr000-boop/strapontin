# F004 · Client reviews of the mosaic (14 Nov 2026)

## First review (after the three layout studies, F003)
- Mosaïque is the best. Wants larger margins that are not all identical (but not too different), an opened poster that adapts to the screen and is fully visible, light shading behind the information on posters, and a full-page design where filters, search and buttons have a clear, pleasing place. → D024 outcome.

## Second review (after the mosaic was built)
1. Filtering and search are not used automatically where they were (top line and header). They should be a **floating bar at the bottom centre** of the screen, modern, obvious, the place the user goes to directly; the same on phones.
2. Principle stated by the client: **main navigation becomes visual first, deliberate filtering second**. The accent is on playfulness on one side and on the viewer's own motivation on the other: "these people love movies and are looking for something local".
3. Ideas to log for later, with justification: saving filters to the user's profile so the page opens on them (I001); a Letterboxd integration showing on the page whether the user has watched a film, has it on their watchlist, or whether friends have seen it (I002).
4. All films in the test must have posters.
