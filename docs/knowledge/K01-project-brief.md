# K01 · Project brief

**Source:** project description written by the team (provided 14 Nov 2026).
**Reliability:** first-hand statement of intent. Market claims inside it are covered in K06.

## What it says
- Concept: a discovery and booking experience inspired by BookMyShow, adapted to independent cinemas and theatres in Paris.
- The aim is not to invent something missing, but to test whether finding, comparing and booking across independent venues can be made easier.
- The team itself notes that the evidence does not yet prove people struggle with this.

## Limits
- The core user problem is a hypothesis. No user research has been run.

## Contribution to the project

- "Compare" is why prices and venue counts are shown before the film page → D007, D008.
