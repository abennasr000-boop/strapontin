# D003 · French by default, English toggle, English-friendly filter

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 14 Nov 2026

## Context
One of three segments is international residents and tourists (K02); v0 was French only.

## Decision
Interface in French with an FR/EN switch. Titles and venue names stay French. A version filter and tag "English-friendly" marks films in English or with English subtitles. VF/VOSTFR codes are explained.

## Arguments
- K02: an unserved segment otherwise.
- K03: language version is a core French-market filter; uncertainty avoidance asks that the language be known before booking.
- Client choice (language question, 14 Nov).

## Risks / what would change our mind
- Editorial content (critics’ quotes) is not translated.
- We assumed English is the right second language for this segment; not verified.

## Where it shows
Header switch; finder and film-page version filters; legend under the finder.
