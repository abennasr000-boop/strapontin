# K03 · Cultural adaptation, France vs India (Hofstede)

**Source:** team analysis provided 14 Nov 2026, using Hofstede's country scores. BookMyShow (India) is the reference product being adapted.
**Reliability:** scores are quoted as given in the brief and were **not re-checked against the Hofstede database by us**; published scores vary by edition. Hofstede describes national averages, not individuals, and is contested as a design tool. Use it to generate hypotheses, not to prove them.

## What it says and what we did with it

| Dimension (FR vs IN, as given) | Implication stated in the brief | Concrete effect in the prototype | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Uncertainty avoidance 86 vs 40 | Full price, exact start/end time, language version and refund policy at every step; no urgency tactics | Price on every ticket; "fin ≈" time; version tag; exchange/refund line in header strip, booking sidebar and summary step; no countdowns or stock warnings | D007, D009, D010, D005 |
| Individualism 74 vs 24 | Personal curation instead of crowd signals; default 1–2 tickets | Bookmark, follow venue/season, "Ma liste"; default quantity 1, cap 6 | D011 |
| Achievement 43 vs 56 | No box-office rankings or "trending"; critics, laurels, debates | Critics' quotes, festival laurels, programmers' picks, special screenings section | D010 |
| Long-term orientation 60 vs 51 | Loyalty features; heritage cinema | Carnet tariff in booking and its own section; seasons and retrospectives that can be followed | D011 |
| Indulgence 48 vs 26 | The outing as a restrained pleasure | Each venue names its bar/café; the e-ticket suggests it in one line | D015 (tone) |

## French market specifics (same source)

## Limits
