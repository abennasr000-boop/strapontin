# K05 · Mood board (Cosmos)

**Source:** https://www.cosmos.so/p0tat/ux (nine pins, read 14 Nov 2026) plus the client's spoken interpretation of it (F001).
**Reliability:** primary source for the intended feeling. Interpretation is subjective; the client's words in F001 override ours.

## What is on it
1. Palette card: Blue Heaven #90ADBF, Vintage Wine #3F1921 (sampled #3F1921), Golden Green (sampled #8E6D06), Orangeade (sampled #D6411F), Raspberry (sampled #7D222F), Cream (sampled #EEE1D1).
2. Animated geometric pattern poster with a large serif wordmark ("OKCA").
3. Blank instant-photo frames with tape.
4. A 3×3 grid of dim, grainy concert polaroids.
5. A cream page with a few small photos and a lot of empty space.
6. A beige "grid system" brand sheet: hairline grid, black-and-white photos, elegant serif with italics.
7–9. Two gallery websites on white (tiny thumbnails; hovering reveals a large image) and a dark editorial page with scattered small photos.

## How we read it, and how that changed
- **v1 reading (wrong weighting):** we leaned on pin 2 (pattern) and the palette at full saturation → bold, graphic, "cartoony". See D014.
- **Client correction (F001):** the north star is pin 6 plus the two gallery videos; pin 2 is explicitly *not* the reference. Feeling: film grain, haze, polaroid nostalgia, bohemian Paris, lightness, dreaminess, discovery.

## Contribution to the project
- Palette values → colour tokens (D015, D017).
- Pins 3, 4 → instant-photo frame as the main image container (D015).
- Pin 6 → hairline rules, margin labels, light serif with italics, no boxes (D015).
- Gallery videos → thumbnail wall with hover reveal on the homepage (D016).
