# K02 · Problem, segments, tasks, constraints, success factor

**Source:** team working document (Dani, Abi, Harshit, Daniel Alex Irudayaraj), provided 14 Nov 2026.
**Reliability:** team definitions, not validated with users.

## What it says
- **Problem (HMW):** make it easier for people in Paris to discover, compare and book films at independent cinemas through one central platform.
- **Segments:** Parisian art-house/indie film-goers · young adults and university students · international residents and tourists.
- **Tasks:** discover venues and films · browse and compare screenings, schedules, locations, prices · review details · complete booking.
- **Technical constraint:** no card numbers stored on the site; payment through a certified gateway such as Stripe.
- **Critical success factor:** engagement rate.

## Limits
- Segments are not sized or prioritised. "Engagement rate" has no agreed definition yet (return visits? saves? bookings per visitor?).

## Contribution to the project
- Three segments → three needs the identity must answer (identity guide §2) → D003, D015.
- International segment → EN toggle, "English-friendly" filter, explained version codes → D003.
- Students deciding late on a phone → finder, price on every ticket, mobile layouts → D006, D007.
- Engagement rate → bookmark, follow, "Ma liste", carnet → D011. It is also the yardstick for D016.
- Payment constraint → D013.
