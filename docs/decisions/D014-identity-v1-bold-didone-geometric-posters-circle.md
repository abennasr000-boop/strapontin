# D014 · Identity v1: bold Didone, geometric posters, circled word, seat mark

**Status:** Superseded by D015
**Date:** 14 Nov 2026

## Context
First translation of the mood board (K05) into an identity.

## Decision
Bodoni Moda display, DM Mono data, thick outlines with hard offset shadows, generated geometric posters, a hand-circled headline word, and a logo made of a bar and a half-disc.

## Arguments
- K05, with the patterned poster pin weighted heavily.
- Judgement: maximum clarity of actions.

## Risks / what would change our mind
- Realised (F001): too bold and cartoony; logo unreadable as a seat and close to an existing brand; circled word hurt readability; palette too saturated.
