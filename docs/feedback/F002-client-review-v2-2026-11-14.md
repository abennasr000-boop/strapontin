# F002 · Client review of v2 (14 Nov 2026)

**From:** the client, after opening prototype v2. A reference image was attached: the beige "grid system / Here's another lockup with some Pretty photos" brand sheet from the mood board (K05, pin 6).

## What was said
1. Opened quickly, the page has too much information and too many textures, and does not guide to the essential.
2. The opening statement ("Les salles indépendantes…") takes too much space for nothing.
3. The polaroid light and rendering are heavy on the eye.
4. The bold aspects sit in an awkward in-between: not dreamy enough, not clear enough.
5. Replace the generated stills with real film images and posters (critic-awarded art-house films). Mockup only, not to be published; a real view is needed.
6. Boost the simpler, gallery-like feeling, which even the original Lovable version had more of.
7. Exploring visually is interesting: the page should open on a gallery of films available, with the clear information decided earlier, clear filters at the top (date, place…), and a "voir toutes les séances" button leading to the classic list view. Make it refined.
8. The font combination makes little sense and feels weak: use somewhat bolder fonts, taking the attached reference as the model for fonts and overall design.
9. This concerns mobile interfaces above all. **The main goal is playful discovery of films across venues**, with clear information and quick filters.

D015 superseded by D022 · D016 superseded by D021 · D017 closed by D022 · D023 created (real posters in the mockup) · K05 updated.
