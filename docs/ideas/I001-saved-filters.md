# I001 · Saved filters: the home page opens on your own view

**Raised by:** client, F004 (14 Nov 2026) · **Status:** logged, not built

## Idea
A signed-in user can save a filter set (for example "ce soir · 11e and 20e · VO") to their profile. The home page then opens directly on it. Several named sets could exist ("près du bureau", "week-end").

## Why it could matter
- **Engagement rate (K02):** a page that already knows your neighbourhood and language removes the first two taps of every visit and gives a reason to come back to *this* site instead of a venue's own.
- **Local intent (F004):** "these people love movies and are looking for something local". Locality is stable for a person; asking for it every time is friction.
- **Individualism, personal curation (K03):** fits the brief's preference for personal features over crowd signals.
- **Proximity (K06, CNC as cited):** 75% consider distance; a saved area makes that the default.
- **International segment (K02):** "English-friendly" saved once.

## What it needs
- Accounts (the prototype's list is device-only today). A first step without accounts: remember the last filters on the device.
- A visible, reversible state: the title line must say the page opened on saved filters, with one tap to see everything.
- Consent and privacy wording: a saved area is personal data (GDPR).

## Risks
- A saved filter narrows the gallery and works against discovery, the main goal (D021). Mitigation: show "outside your filters" tiles at the end, or a discreet "tout Paris" switch.
- Stale preferences (people move, change habits).

## Open questions
- Default to the saved view, or offer it as a first chip in the dock?
- Does this belong with alerts for followed venues and seasons (D011)?
