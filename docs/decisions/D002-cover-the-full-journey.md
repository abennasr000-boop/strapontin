# D002 · Cover the full journey

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 14 Nov 2026

## Context
The audit (K04) only covered the homepage, but the task list (K02) runs from discovery to completed booking.

## Decision
Prototype home, film page, venue page, season page and the five-step booking flow.

## Arguments
- K01/K02: the product is judged on discover → compare → book, so a homepage alone cannot be evaluated.
- K03: uncertainty-avoidance rules (price, times, refund at every step) only become testable across the whole flow.
- Client choice (scope question, 14 Nov).

## Risks / what would change our mind
- More surface to keep consistent at each identity change.

## Where it shows
All routes in `site/index.html`.
