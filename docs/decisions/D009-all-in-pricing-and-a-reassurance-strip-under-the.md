# D009 · All-in pricing and a reassurance strip under the header

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 14 Nov 2026

## Context
K04 C3: the strongest trust statements were in the footer.

## Decision
A strip under the header states all-in prices, free exchange up to two hours before, Pass Culture and reduced fares. The same facts repeat in the booking sidebar and summary.

## Arguments
- K03: uncertainty avoidance.
- K04 C3.

## Risks / what would change our mind
- These are promises the real service must be able to keep (exchange policy depends on venues).

## Where it shows
Header strip; booking steps 1, 3, 4.
